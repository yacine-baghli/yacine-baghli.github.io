Research

Master's Thesis · CentraleSupélec / IETR (UMR CNRS 6164)

TraceDiff

Discrete Diffusion Models for Structured Noise Understanding

A discrete diffusion model that learns the structure of noise — insertions, deletions, and substitutions — rather than treating corruption as generic. Built an alignment-marginalizing encoder with attention pooling over traces and alignment hypotheses, enabling robust fusion of noisy observations. Achieves state-of-the-art on the Clustered Nanopore Reads benchmark, outperforming prior methods (TrellisBMA) on edit distance and failure rate while maintaining constant-time inference. Full training stack built from scratch: mixed-precision (BF16), curriculum noise scheduling, systematic ablation studies across 5 architectural dimensions.

First-author manuscript in preparation — targeting workshop submission (NeurIPS/ICML)

Single-Cell Computational Biology for Autoimmune Diseases

Building a T-cell atlas for Multiple Sclerosis by integrating longitudinal scRNA-seq mouse datasets (EAE model) with public human MS datasets. Developing computational pipelines for single-cell data analysis: quality control, batch correction, clustering (Leiden), differential expression, and trajectory analysis. Collaborating with immunologists on clinically grounded ML — translating computational findings into experimentally testable hypotheses about T-cell subpopulations and immune checkpoint (VISTA) dynamics.
